Therapy

Therapy is the work beneath the surface — the mental health challenges, old wounds, and long-held beliefs that get in the way of real healing. We'll look at what's happening now and where it traces back to, using evidence-based assessments to build a treatment plan we keep revisiting. There's no set finish line: some people are in and out in a season, others stay with it for years, and both are valid. Healing isn't a race — it's a process. Lean into it.

03

Life Coaching

Life coaching is present-and-future work — a focused conversation about a specific goal, built around your existing strengths rather than a diagnosis. We'll map out where you want to end up and work through what's standing in the way. Once you reach that goal, the engagement wraps up — unless a new one shows up, in which case we set a new plan and go again. It's meant to be shorter-term by design.

Good to Know

Good Faith Estimate Notice

Since January 2022, federal law has required health care providers to give patients who are uninsured, or who choose not to use insurance, a written estimate of expected charges — including psychotherapy — before a service is scheduled.

You have the right to request a Good Faith Estimate for the total expected cost of any non-emergency care, from me or from any other provider you choose. If your final bill comes in $400 or more above that estimate, you're entitled to dispute it — so it's worth holding onto a copy of your estimate.

Questions about your right to a Good Faith Estimate? Call 800-985-3059, visit cms.gov/nosurprises, or just ask — I'm glad to walk through it with you.
