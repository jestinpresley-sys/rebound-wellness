From the Court to the Couch

The Journey

My path into this work started on a roster, not in a classroom. Here's how a couple of decades around competition turned into a private practice built for people who compete — with themselves as much as anyone else.

2015
First steps in mental health

Began working professionally with families and children navigating autism, across the Killeen and North Austin area.

2016 – 2020
Every level of care

Worked with at-risk youth, in inpatient mental health hospitals for both children and adults, and across outpatient programs — including IOP, PHP, and substance-use treatment — throughout Central Texas.

2020
Coaching athletes, on and off the field

Started serving as a life coach for student-athletes and program alumni through Memphis Inner City Rugby (MICR).

Now
Building The Rebound Wellness

Working toward full licensure while growing a private practice that combines life coaching and therapy — with a goal of joining insurance panels and, longer term, building free resources for anyone trying to find the right kind of support.
