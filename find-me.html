Find Me

Places with my presence.

Rebound Wellness is only one room I show up in. Here's where else you'll find me, and the people building alongside me.

01

Thru The Game

A program built by my friend Kathryn, a fellow therapist based in Colorado, focused on bringing mental health awareness to student-athletes nationwide. Her work traces the same throughline mine does — using sport as the entry point into deeper conversations.

Visit Thru The Game
02

Memphis Inner City Rugby (MICR)

The community where my life-coaching work started. MICR supports Memphis students and their goals through rugby — athletes who become coaches who become champions, reshaping both the sport and their own futures along the way.

Visit MICR
03

ShootersXShoot

Founded by BriAnna, one of my favorite people on the planet. She's spent the last couple of years teaching teams and players how to evaluate and refine their basketball shot — and along the way, the discipline, courage, and confidence that come with it. Skills that hold up whether or not a ball's in your hands.

Visit ShootersXShoot
